# Halo mass functions from maximum entropy distributions in collisionless dark matter flow

Xu Z., 2026, *Halo mass functions from maximum entropy distributions in collisionless dark matter flow*, Zenodo [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24095863](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24095863). The preprint is hosted on arXiv as [arXiv:2110.09676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.09676). This record is a new version of the earlier record [zenodo.org/records/6640325](https://zenodo.org/records/6640325).

## Major findings

- The velocity distribution that maximises the entropy of a self-gravitating collisionless system (the X distribution) is a scale mixture of Gaussians of halo groups; without halo motion its mixing distribution is exactly the generalised inverse Gaussian distribution H∞.
- H∞ is a maximum entropy distribution only for potential exponent n = 0, not for gravity.
- A Gaussian mixture built from the measured halo-particle dispersions reproduces the velocity distribution of the halo particles to 0.03 dex (rms over |v| < 6σ₀) and its kurtosis (5.10 against 5.12), yet no halo particle has a total dispersion below 0.51σ₀², where H∞ places 0.28–0.34 of the particles.
- For any halo velocity dispersion bounded away from zero, the exact equation for the mass function has no solution; its push-forward solution leaves a fraction 0.27 of the mixture unassigned.
- The simulated mass function in ν = σᵥ²/σ̄ₕ² is described by the two-parameter P form with rms 0.18 in ln f, comparable to the fitted double-λ (0.18) and Sheth–Tormen (0.21) forms.
- The mass function is not independent of redshift: the mean ν rises from 0.56 at z = 2 to 1.14 at z = 0.
